# 🌙 10 Bedtime & Children’s Story Soundtracks (Suno AI Prompts)

This guide contains **10 tested, ultra-soothing Suno AI instrumental prompts** designed specifically for children's bedtime stories, fairytale narration, and sleep audiobooks in **FableVoice Studio**.

---

## ⚙️ Suno AI Setup Instructions (For 100% Pure Instrumental Music):
1. Open [Suno.com](https://suno.com) ➔ Click **Create**.
2. Turn **Custom Mode** ➔ **ON**.
3. Turn **Instrumental** ➔ **ON** *(Ensures zero singing or vocals)*.
4. Copy and paste the **Style of Music** and **Structure Tags** below into Suno.
5. Click **Generate** ➔ Download the `.mp3`/`.wav` and drop it into **FableVoice Ambience Library**!
