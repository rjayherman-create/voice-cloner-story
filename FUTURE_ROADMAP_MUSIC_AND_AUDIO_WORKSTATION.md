# 🎵 Future Product Roadmap: Music Library & Narration Audio Editing Workstation

---

## 📌 Executive Vision
Transform **FableVoice Studio** from an AI voice generator into a **Full Creator Audio Workstation (Micro-DAW)** with multi-category royalty-free music, user audio uploads, smart auto-ducking, intro/outro music tails, and stem exports.

## 2. 🎚️ Narration & Music Audio Editing Capabilities

### Visual 2-Track Audio Timeline:
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🎙️ Track 1 (Voiceover):   [ "Welcome to today's episode..." ] ─── [ "Our main topic is..." ]           │
│ 🎵 Track 2 (Soundtrack):  [ ── Intro Music Fade-in ── ] ─────── [ Auto-Ducked (15%) ] ──── [ Fade-out ]│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 🎛️ Studio Controls:                                                                                    │
│   • 🦆 Smart Auto-Ducking (Lowers music volume automatically when voice is speaking)                   │
│   • ✂️ Silence Trimmer (1-click remove long awkward pauses)                                             │
│   • ⏱️ Intro / Outro Tail (Let music play for 3 seconds before voice starts & after it ends)          │
│   • 📦 Multi-Track Stem Exports (Download "Full Mix", "Voice-Only", or "Music-Only" stems)             │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Core Editing Tools:
- **🦆 Auto-Ducking**: Automatically attenuates background music to `15%` when voiceover is detected, and raises it back to `35%` during dramatic pauses.
- **⏱️ Intro / Outro Music Tails**: Configure 3 to 5 seconds of clean intro music before narration starts and outro fade-outs after narration finishes.
- **✂️ Waveform Silence Trimmer**: Clean leading/trailing silence or adjust inter-sentence pause length.
- **📦 Stem Export Package**: Download individual uncompressed audio stems (`Voiceover_Only.mp3`, `Soundtrack_Only.mp3`, and `Master_Broadcast_Mix.mp3`).
