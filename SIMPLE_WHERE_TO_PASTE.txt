# 🎯 ULTRA-SIMPLE: Where To Paste Your Key

## The Prompt You'll See

```
🔑 Enter your ElevenLabs API key (hidden): ___
                                            👆 PASTE HERE
```

## What To Do

1. **Get your key** from https://elevenlabs.io/account/api-keys
2. **Copy it** (click the [Copy] button)
3. **Click in PowerShell** where you see the cursor
4. **Right-click** → **Paste** (or Ctrl+V)
5. **See dots appear**: `••••••••••••••••••••••••••••••`
6. **Press Enter**
7. **Type**: `yes`
8. **Press Enter**
9. **Done!** ✅

---

## That's It!

Your key is now safely saved and hidden behind dots on your screen.

The setup script handles everything else. 🚀
