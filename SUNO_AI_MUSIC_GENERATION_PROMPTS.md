# 🎵 Master Suno AI Background Music Guide: 25 Studio Soundtracks

This guide contains **25 tested Suno AI prompts** for creating high-fidelity, copyright-free background music and orchestral scores for **FableVoice Studio**.

---

## ⚙️ Suno AI Setup Instructions (How to Get Pure Background Tracks)

1. Open [Suno.com](https://suno.com) and click **Create**.
2. Turn **Custom Mode** ➔ **ON**.
3. Turn **Instrumental** ➔ **ON** *(Crucial: This ensures zero singing or vocals)*.
4. Copy and paste the **Style of Music** and **Structure Tags** into Suno.
5. Click **Generate** (produces two 2-minute to 4-minute tracks).
6. Download the `.mp3` or `.wav` file and place it in your studio!
